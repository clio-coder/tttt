- 모든 장이 같은 순서다: **한눈에 → 본문(정의 · 정리 · 증명 · 예제) → 자주 틀리는 것 → 셀프 체크**

## 공식 한 장

**01 회귀와 추정**
- $\hat\beta=(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y$ · $\nabla(\beta^\top A\beta)=(A+A^\top)\beta$ · GD $\beta\leftarrow\beta-\alpha\nabla f$
- posterior ∝ likelihood × prior · MAP $=\arg\min[-\log p(x\mid\theta)-\log p(\theta)]$
- 베르누이 + Beta $(a,b)$ → Beta $(a+S,\thinspace b+n-S)$
- Ridge $(\lambda I+X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y$, MAP로 $\lambda=\sigma^2/\tau^2$ · MSE = Bias² + Var
- Kernel ridge $f^\ast(x)=K(x,X)(K+\lambda I)^{-1}y$

**02 정보이론**
- $H(X)=-\mathbb E\log p(X)$ · $H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y\mid X)$
- $KL(p\Vert q)=\mathbb E_p\log\frac pq\ge0$ · $I(X;Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)$ · $H_p(q)=H(p)+KL(p\Vert q)$

**03 분류**
- $\sigma(z)=\frac1{1+e^{-z}}$, $\sigma'=\sigma(1-\sigma)$ · 로지스틱 기울기 $\sum(\sigma(w^\top x_i)-y_i)x_i$, Hessian $X^\top SX$
- softmax $p_k=e^{w_k^\top x}/\sum_je^{w_j^\top x}$, 기울기 $-\sum_i(y_{im}-p_m)x_{in}$
- 거리 $\lvert w^\top x+b\rvert/\lVert w\rVert$ · SVM $\min\frac12\lVert w\rVert^2$ s.t. $y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge1$ · $w=\sum\alpha_iy_ix_i$, $\sum\alpha_iy_i=0$, $b=y_s-x_s^\top w$

**04 신경망과 역전파**
- downstream = upstream × local · add = 그대로, mul = 상대 입력, copy = 더함, max = 최댓값 쪽만

**05 학습 기술**
- $\mathbb E[\nabla f_i]=\nabla f$ · Xavier $\sigma^2=1/D_{in}$ · He $\sigma^2=2/D_{in}$
- BN $\hat x=\frac{x-\mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2+\varepsilon}}$, $y=\gamma\hat x+\beta$ · cosine $\alpha_t=\frac12\alpha_0(1+\cos(t\pi/T))$

**06 최적화**
- $f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+\frac\beta2\lVert y-x\rVert^2$ · GD 감소 $\eta\lt2/\beta$
- $\min_k\mathbb E\lVert\nabla f(x_k)\rVert^2\le\frac{f(x_0)-f_\ast}{\eta T}+\frac{LG}2\eta$
- momentum $v\leftarrow\rho v-\alpha\nabla f(x)$ · Nesterov $\nabla f(x+\rho v)$ · AdaGrad $\alpha/(\sqrt{A}+\epsilon)$ · Adam $\hat m=m/(1-\beta^t)$

**07 CNN**
- $N'=\frac{N-K+2P}S+1$ · 파라미터 $(K^2C+1)D$ · same padding $P=\frac{K-1}2$
- equivariant $f(g\cdot x)=g\cdot f(x)$ · residual $y=x+F(x)$, $\frac{\partial y}{\partial x}=I+\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}$ · saliency $\partial S_c(I)/\partial I$
